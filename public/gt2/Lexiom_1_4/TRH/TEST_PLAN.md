## Known divergences

1. **Document Realization is server-composed** in Phase b/c POC (not in-page WebContainer CA). TRH still serves COOP/COEP so the isolation surface is ready; full in-page VAL CA on TRH origin remains Follow-up.
2. **Canonical / Signed** is a TRH attestation record in SoR (not cryptographic PKI).
3. **Metering events** are append-only POC JSONL (`hmac_note: poc_unsigned`), not GTIH-signed tariff/royalty records.
4. **`generateOsn` without GT3 inference** fills the Lexiom-owned document OSN skeleton from the prompt (same fields; not a live LM YAML).
