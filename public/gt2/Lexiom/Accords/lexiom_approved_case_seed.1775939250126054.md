## Case Seed Narrative

My client is currently facing a dispute with their landlord regarding the return of a security deposit. The landlord is withholding a $3,000 deposit, claiming that my client caused damages to the property that exceed normal wear and tear. My client moved out on March 1 and has provided photographic evidence and a walkthrough email to support their position. 

According to the lease agreement, the landlord is required to return the deposit within 30 days of my client's move-out date. However, the landlord has not complied with this requirement and has ignored multiple requests for the refund. 

In the landlord's initial reply, they assert that the deposit was only $2,000 and that they found several issues during their inspection after my client vacated the premises. The landlord mentions damages that they believe justify withholding the deposit, including problems with the wooden kitchen, and states that they are in the process of gathering invoices and estimates to document these costs.

At this point, my client seeks to send a demand letter to the landlord to formally request the return of the security deposit. Should this matter remain unresolved, my client is prepared to pursue action in small claims court to recover the funds.
