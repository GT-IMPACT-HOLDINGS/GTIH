Security Deposit Dispute Resolution

## Common Ground
Both parties acknowledge that the landlord is withholding a $2,000 security deposit, and the lease explicitly states that the deposit must be returned within 30 days of the tenant's move-out date, which occurred on March 1. There is a mutual understanding that the tenant has made multiple requests for the return of the deposit.

## Clarifiable Differences
The landlord claims damages that the tenant asserts were not caused by them, leading to the withholding of the deposit. The landlord may argue that he has provided reasons for this action, while the tenant feels that their refund requests have been ignored. Additionally, the interpretation of the lease terms regarding the timeline for returning the deposit may be a point of contention.

## Undisputed Anchors
- The landlord is withholding the $2,000 security deposit.
- The lease states that the deposit must be returned within 30 days.
- The tenant moved out on March 1.
- The landlord has ignored the tenant's refund requests.
- The tenant did not take photos of the property upon moving out.

## Invitation Direction
As both parties seek resolution, it is important to engage in open communication and collaboration to address the concerns regarding the deposit. A structured approach to discussing the alleged damages and the timeline for the deposit return can foster understanding and lead to a mutually agreeable outcome.
