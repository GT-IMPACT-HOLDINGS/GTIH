Security Deposit Dispute

## Common Ground
Both parties acknowledge that the landlord is currently withholding the tenant's $2,000 security deposit. The lease agreement specifies that the deposit must be returned within 30 days of the tenant's move-out date, which occurred on March 1. The tenant has made multiple requests for the refund, indicating a desire to resolve the matter amicably.

## Clarifiable Differences
The primary point of contention lies in the landlord's assertion of damages allegedly caused by the tenant, which the tenant disputes. The landlord may have evidence supporting their claims, while the tenant maintains they are not responsible for any damages. Additionally, the landlord's response to the refund requests is unclear, as the tenant feels ignored, whereas the landlord might argue they have provided justifiable reasons for withholding the deposit.

## Undisputed Anchors
- The landlord is withholding the security deposit.
- The lease states the deposit must be returned within 30 days.
- The tenant moved out on March 1.
- The tenant has made refund requests.
- The tenant did not take photos upon moving out.

## Invitation Direction
In the spirit of collaboration, it is encouraged that both parties engage in open communication to clarify the situation regarding the alleged damages and the deposit return. This approach can foster a constructive dialogue aimed at reaching a resolution that satisfies both the tenant's financial needs and the landlord's concerns.
