Tenant Security Deposit Dispute

## Common Ground
1. Both parties acknowledge that the Tenant vacated the premises on March 1, establishing a clear timeline for the return of the security deposit.
2. The lease agreement stipulates that the security deposit must be returned within 30 days of vacating, which sets the deadline for return as March 31.
3. The Tenant documented the condition of the property through photographs and a walkthrough email, which serves as a basis for assessing the state of the premises at the time of vacating.

## Clarifiable Differences
1. The Tenant asserts that the security deposit amounted to $3,000, whereas the Landlord claims it was only $2,000, creating a factual dispute regarding the amount of the deposit.
2. The Landlord alleges that damages requiring repair, cleaning, and replacement exceed ordinary wear and tear, while the Tenant contends that any issues fall within the scope of normal wear and tear.
3. The Landlord has indicated that damages to the wooden kitchen were not present at the time of the Tenant's move-in, a claim that the Tenant disputes, asserting that such damages did not exist at that time.
4. The Landlord may argue that the security deposit is being withheld due to the alleged damages, which introduces a potential dispute regarding the obligation to return the deposit.

## Undisputed Anchors
1. The Tenant vacated the premises on March 1.
2. The lease agreement stipulates that the security deposit must be returned within 30 days.
3. The Tenant documented the condition of the property through photographs and a walkthrough email.
4. The Tenant has made multiple requests for the return of the security deposit.
5. The Landlord is in the process of gathering invoices and estimates to substantiate claimed costs.

## Invitation Direction
In light of the aforementioned points, it is respectfully suggested that both parties engage in a constructive dialogue to resolve the security deposit dispute amicably. The Tenant is prepared to issue a formal demand letter for the return of the security deposit. Should the Landlord fail to comply, the Tenant is willing to pursue a small claims action to recover the withheld funds. A resolution that acknowledges the Tenant's rights under the lease agreement would be beneficial for both parties.
