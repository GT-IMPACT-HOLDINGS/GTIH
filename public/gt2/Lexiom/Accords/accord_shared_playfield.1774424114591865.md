Security Deposit Dispute Resolution

## Common Ground
Both parties recognize that the landlord is currently withholding the tenant's $2000 security deposit. The lease agreement clearly states that the deposit must be returned within 30 days of the tenant's move-out date, which occurred on March 1. There is also mutual acknowledgment that the tenant did not take photos of the property upon moving out.

## Clarifiable Differences
The primary contention lies in the landlord's claim of damages allegedly caused by the tenant, which the tenant disputes. While the landlord may argue that the return of the deposit is contingent upon assessing these damages, the tenant maintains that the lease terms are explicit regarding the timeline for deposit return. Additionally, the landlord's response to the tenant's refund requests is in question, with the tenant feeling that these requests have been ignored, while the landlord may assert that they have addressed them.

## Undisputed Anchors
- The landlord is withholding the security deposit.
- The lease states the deposit must be returned within 30 days.
- The tenant moved out on March 1.
- The tenant did not take photos of the property.
- The landlord has ignored the tenant's refund requests.

## Invitation Direction
In light of these points, it is encouraged for both parties to engage in a constructive dialogue to clarify the situation surrounding the deposit. A professional tone in communication can facilitate a resolution that respects both the tenant's rights and the landlord's responsibilities.
