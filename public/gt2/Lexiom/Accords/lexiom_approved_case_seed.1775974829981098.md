## Case Seed Narrative

On behalf of my client, we address the dispute regarding the security deposit associated with the rental property. The claim presented by the tenant states that the security deposit amounts to $3,000; however, my client asserts that the correct amount is $2,000.

Following the tenant's move-out date of March 1, my client conducted a thorough inspection of the premises. This inspection revealed multiple issues requiring repair, cleaning, and replacement that exceed ordinary wear and tear. Notably, the condition of the wooden kitchen was found to be significantly below the standard expected, with damages that were not present at the time of move-in.

In light of these findings, my client believes that the conditions observed justify the withholding of the security deposit. Furthermore, my client is actively gathering invoices and estimates to substantiate the costs associated with the necessary repairs and cleaning.

We seek to clarify these points and resolve the matter amicably, ensuring that all parties have a clear understanding of the situation.
