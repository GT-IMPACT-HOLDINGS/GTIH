Security Deposit Dispute

## Common Ground
Both parties acknowledge that the landlord is withholding a $2000 security deposit and that the lease stipulates this deposit must be returned within 30 days of the tenant moving out. The tenant moved out on March 1 and has made multiple requests for the refund, which have not been addressed by the landlord.

## Clarifiable Differences
The primary point of contention lies in the landlord's claim of damages allegedly caused by the tenant, which the tenant disputes. Additionally, while the tenant asserts that the landlord has ignored refund requests, the landlord may argue that they are addressing the situation or have valid reasons for the delay. The absence of photos documenting the property's condition at move-out is also a point of difference, as it affects the evidence available to both parties.

## Undisputed Anchors
- The landlord is withholding a $2000 security deposit.
- The lease states that the deposit must be returned within 30 days.
- The tenant moved out on March 1.
- The tenant has requested a refund multiple times.
- The tenant did not take photos of the property upon moving out.

## Invitation Direction
In the spirit of collaboration, it is encouraged that both parties engage in open communication to resolve this matter amicably. The tenant seeks a clear acknowledgment of the situation and the return of the deposit, while the landlord may provide their perspective on the alleged damages. A constructive dialogue could lead to a mutually agreeable resolution.
