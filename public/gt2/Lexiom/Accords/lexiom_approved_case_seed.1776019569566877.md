## Case Seed Narrative

### Parties Involved
- **Tenant:** My client
- **Landlord:** Unnamed individual

### Background
My client asserts that the landlord is unlawfully withholding a security deposit of $3,000. The landlord contends that the deposit was only $2,000 and claims damages incurred during my client's tenancy that justify the retention of the deposit.

### Relevant Facts
1. **Move-Out Date:** My client vacated the premises on March 1.
2. **Deposit Amount:** My client claims a security deposit of $3,000, while the landlord asserts it was $2,000.
3. **Condition of Premises:** 
   - My client provided photographic evidence and a walkthrough email prior to vacating the property.
   - The landlord conducted an inspection post-move-out and reported multiple issues requiring repair, cleaning, and replacement beyond ordinary wear and tear, specifically mentioning damage to the wooden kitchen.
4. **Communication:** My client has made several requests for the return of the security deposit, which the landlord has not addressed substantively, citing ongoing efforts to gather documentation for the alleged damages.

### Legal Considerations
- **Lease Agreement:** The lease stipulates that the security deposit must be returned within 30 days of move-out.
- **Damages:** The landlord's claims of damages must be substantiated with appropriate documentation, including invoices and estimates, which have not yet been provided.

### Next Steps
My client seeks to initiate a demand letter to the landlord for the return of the security deposit. Should the landlord fail to respond adequately, my client is prepared to pursue a small claims action to recover the amount owed.
