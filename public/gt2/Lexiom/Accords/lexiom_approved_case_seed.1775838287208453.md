## Case Seed Narrative

The tenant has reported that their landlord is withholding a security deposit of $3,000, claiming damages that the tenant asserts they did not cause. The tenant moved out on March 1 and provided photographic evidence and a walkthrough email to support their position. Despite multiple requests for the return of the deposit, the landlord has not responded satisfactorily. According to the lease agreement, the deposit must be returned within 30 days of move-out.

In response, the landlord disputes the tenant's claim, stating that the original deposit was only $2,000, not $3,000. The landlord claims to have found multiple issues requiring repair, cleaning, and replacement beyond normal wear and tear during their inspection after the tenant's departure. Specific mention was made of damages to the wooden kitchen, which the landlord asserts were not present at the time of move-in. The landlord also indicated that they are currently gathering invoices and estimates to substantiate their claims regarding the costs of repairs.

The tenant is seeking a demand letter to request the return of the deposit and is prepared to pursue small-claims action if necessary.
