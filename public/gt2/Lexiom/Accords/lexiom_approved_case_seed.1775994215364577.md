## Case Seed Narrative

### Parties Involved
- **Tenant**: My client, hereinafter referred to as "the Tenant."
- **Landlord**: The opposing party, hereinafter referred to as "the Landlord."

### Background
1. The Tenant entered into a lease agreement with the Landlord, which included a security deposit of $3,000.
2. The Tenant vacated the premises on March 1. Prior to vacating, the Tenant documented the condition of the property through photographs and a walkthrough email.

### Dispute
3. The Landlord has withheld the Tenant's security deposit, claiming that the amount provided was only $2,000, contrary to the Tenant's assertion of $3,000.
4. The Landlord further alleges that upon inspection of the premises after the Tenant's departure, multiple issues were identified that required repair, cleaning, and replacement, which the Landlord asserts exceed ordinary wear and tear. Specific reference was made to damages involving the wooden kitchen, which the Landlord claims were not present at the time of the Tenant's move-in.
5. The Landlord has indicated that they are in the process of gathering invoices and estimates to substantiate the claimed costs associated with the alleged damages.

### Legal Obligations
6. The lease agreement stipulates that the security deposit must be returned to the Tenant within 30 days of vacating the premises. Given that the Tenant vacated on March 1, the deadline for the return of the deposit would have been March 31.

### Current Status
7. The Tenant has made multiple requests for the return of the security deposit, all of which have been disregarded by the Landlord.

### Next Steps
8. The Tenant seeks a formal demand letter to be issued to the Landlord regarding the return of the security deposit. Should the Landlord fail to comply, the Tenant is prepared to initiate a small claims action to recover the withheld funds.

### Conclusion
9. The Tenant maintains that the withholding of the security deposit is unjustified and seeks resolution through appropriate legal channels.
