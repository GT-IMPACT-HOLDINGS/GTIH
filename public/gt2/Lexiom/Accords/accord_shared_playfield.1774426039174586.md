Security Deposit Dispute

## Common Ground
Both parties acknowledge that the landlord is withholding a $2,000 security deposit, which is a central issue in this case. Additionally, it is agreed that the lease stipulates the deposit must be returned within 30 days of the tenant's move-out date, which occurred on March 1. The tenant has made multiple requests for the refund, indicating a willingness to resolve the matter amicably.

## Clarifiable Differences
The landlord claims that damages were caused by the tenant, which the tenant disputes, asserting that they are not responsible for any alleged damages. The landlord may argue that the conditions for returning the deposit have not been met, while the tenant maintains that the lease terms require a timely return of the deposit. There is also a discrepancy regarding the communication of refund requests, with the tenant stating these were ignored and the landlord potentially claiming they were addressed.

## Undisputed Anchors
- The landlord is withholding a $2,000 security deposit.
- The lease states that the deposit must be returned within 30 days.
- The tenant moved out on March 1.
- The tenant has requested a refund multiple times.
- The landlord claims damages were caused by the tenant.

## Invitation Direction
In light of the situation, it is encouraged that both parties engage in a constructive dialogue to clarify the differing perspectives regarding the alleged damages and the conditions for the return of the deposit. A collaborative approach may lead to a resolution that respects the rights and obligations outlined in the lease agreement.
