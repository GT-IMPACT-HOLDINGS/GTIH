## Common Ground
Both parties acknowledge that the security deposit amount is $2,000, and that the tenant vacated the apartment on March 1. The lease agreement clearly states that the deposit must be returned within 30 days of vacating, which establishes a mutual understanding of the timeline for the return of funds.

## Clarifiable Differences
The landlord claims damages that the tenant asserts were not caused by them, leading to the current dispute over the return of the deposit. The tenant has provided documentation of the move-out inspection, which the landlord has not addressed. The differing perspectives on the cause of damages and the lack of communication from the landlord are areas that require further discussion.

- Lease agreement states deposit must be returned within 30 days.
