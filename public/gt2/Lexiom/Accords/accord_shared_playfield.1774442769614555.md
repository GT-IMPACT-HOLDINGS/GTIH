Security Deposit Dispute Resolution

## Common Ground
Both parties acknowledge that the landlord is withholding a $2000 security deposit, which is a significant issue for the tenant. The tenant moved out on March 1, and the lease stipulates that the deposit must be returned within 30 days. The tenant has made multiple requests for the refund, which the landlord has not addressed.

## Clarifiable Differences
The landlord claims damages that the tenant asserts were not caused by them, leading to a factual dispute regarding responsibility for these damages. Additionally, while the tenant feels ignored in their refund requests, the landlord may argue that they have provided responses or justifications for withholding the deposit. The interpretation of the lease terms and the timeline for returning the deposit are also points of contention.

## Undisputed Anchors
- The landlord is withholding a $2000 security deposit.
- The damages claimed were not caused by the tenant.
- The tenant moved out on March 1.
- The lease states the deposit must be returned within 30 days.
- The tenant has made refund requests.
- The landlord is ignoring these refund requests.

## Invitation Direction
In light of the current situation, it is essential to approach the resolution process with respect and neutrality. Both parties are encouraged to engage in open communication to clarify misunderstandings and work towards a mutually agreeable solution regarding the security deposit and the alleged damages.
