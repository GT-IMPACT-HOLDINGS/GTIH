## Case Seed Narrative

### Parties Involved
1. **Tenant**: The individual represented by my client, who is seeking the return of a security deposit.
2. **Landlord**: The individual who has withheld the security deposit and disputes the tenant's claims.

### Background
- My client vacated the rental premises on March 1. 
- Upon moving out, my client documented the condition of the premises through photographs and a walkthrough email.
- The lease agreement stipulates that the security deposit must be returned within 30 days of vacating the property.

### Dispute
- The landlord claims that the security deposit provided by my client was $2,000, contrary to my client's assertion of a $3,000 deposit.
- The landlord alleges that upon inspection of the premises, multiple issues were identified that necessitated repairs, cleaning, and replacements beyond normal wear and tear. Specifically, the landlord cited damages to the wooden kitchen and other items not present at the time of my client's move-in.
- The landlord has indicated that he is in the process of gathering invoices and estimates to substantiate the claims of damages.

### Current Status
- My client has made several requests for the return of the security deposit, all of which have been ignored by the landlord.
- My client seeks to initiate a demand letter for the return of the security deposit and, if necessary, pursue a small claims action to recover the funds.

### Conclusion
- The matter requires further legal action to address the landlord's withholding of the security deposit, based on the terms of the lease and the documented evidence provided by my client.
