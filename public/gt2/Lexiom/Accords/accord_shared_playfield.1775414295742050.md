## Common Ground
Both parties acknowledge that the landlord is withholding the $2,000 security deposit, which is a clear and undisputed fact. Additionally, the lease explicitly states that the deposit must be returned within 30 days, establishing a legal obligation for the landlord. Furthermore, it is recognized that the landlord has not responded to the tenant's refund requests, indicating a lack of communication.

## Clarifiable Differences
The primary area of dispute lies in the landlord's claim of damages allegedly caused by the tenant. The tenant contests these claims, asserting that they did not cause any damages. This difference in perspective creates a factual dispute that requires clarification and evidence from both sides to resolve.

## Undisputed Anchors
- The landlord is withholding the $2,000 security deposit.
- The lease states that the deposit must be returned within 30 days.
- The landlord has ignored the tenant's refund requests.

## Invitation Direction
In the spirit of collaboration, it is encouraged for both parties to engage in open communication to address the concerns regarding the alleged damages and the return of the security deposit. By sharing relevant documentation and evidence, a mutual understanding can be reached, facilitating a resolution that respects the rights and obligations of both the tenant and the landlord.
