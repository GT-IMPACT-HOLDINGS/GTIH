# Lexiom Accord Mediation UX Specification
## Version 1.4 — Approval-Gated Entry & Recalculated Shared Seed

---

## 1. Purpose

This specification defines the UX, temporal flow, and semantic mechanics of Lexiom’s Accord stage.

Accord enables structured mediation through:

- staged semantic progression
- turn-based interaction
- explicit mutual approval of shared artifacts
- artifact-based invitation handshake
- re-synthesized shared reality formation
- round-by-round recalculation of the shared case seed

---

## 2. Core Mediation Principle
### Shared Reality Must Be Co-Accepted

Accord begins only when:

> at least one artifact is explicitly accepted by both parties.

Shared narrative is not imposed — it is **emergent from accepted material**.

A mutually approved artifact may be:
- an invitation-derived narrative
- an uploaded case artifact
- a later staged artifact promoted into shared status

---

## 3. Pre-Accord Phase — Asymmetric Handshake

### 3.1 Player A — Proposal Phase

Player A prepares invitation by:

- supplying a **directory path**
  → all files (including subdirectories) become candidate artifacts

- Lexiom processes directory artifacts and:
  - uploads the case directory artifacts to GT3 case storage
  - proposes an **invitation narrative draft**

Player A must:
- edit (optional)
- **approve Lexiom’s invitation narrative**

---

### 3.2 Player B — Acceptance Phase (pre-cockpit rendering)

Player B receives:

- invitation message
- list of candidate artifacts

Capabilities:

- review artifacts
- **approve artifacts (no editing allowed)**
- **approve invitation (no editing allowed)**

---

### 3.3 Accord Entry Condition

Accord begins only when Player B approves:

- at least one artifact, **or**
- the invitation narrative itself

This constitutes:

- first mutual semantic anchor
- explicit willingness to engage
- initial shared material for Accord bootstrapping

---

## 4. Shared Entry Narrative

If the invitation narrative is approved by both players, it is moved to the **shared documents** section.

In that state, it is classified as:

> **Shared Entry Narrative**

The Shared Entry Narrative:

- is a valid shared artifact
- is a valid source for Player B’s initial case-seed proposal
- may serve as the first shared semantic anchor even if no other artifact has yet been co-approved

### 4.1 Semantic Status of Shared Entry Narrative

The Shared Entry Narrative is legitimate shared reality, but with a specific role:

- it establishes a mutually acceptable entry-point
- it does **not** freeze the full case reality permanently
- it may later be refined, contextualized, or superseded by richer shared material

This distinction preserves:
- acceptance legitimacy
- factual flexibility
- mediation-safe forward progress

---

## 5. Shared Reality Formation at Accord Entry

Upon entry, Lexiom infers GT3 for Player B’s **Case Seed Narrative Proposal**.

### Source material:

- all artifacts currently in the **shared** section
- including the Shared Entry Narrative if it exists

### Result:

- a **third-perspective unified narrative**
- appears as a draft awaiting approval in the **staged** documents section within Player B’s cockpit right panel
- is presented in Player B’s center playfield once the cockpit is rendered

---

## 6. Shared vs Staged Documents

### Shared documents

Visible to both players in their **shared** sections.

These are artifacts approved by both parties.

### Staged documents

Visible as artifacts approved by one player and awaiting approval of the other.

These include:
- Lexiom-proposed drafts
- player-bounded approved artifacts awaiting counterpart approval
- recalculated case-seed proposals for the next round

System communicates:

> “You are now in a shared semantic space.”

---

## 7. Draft-First Glyph System

All **shared** artifacts include glyph state:

- Approved: ring with check (◯ with ✓ rendered inside the ring)

All **staged** artifacts include glyph state:

- Proposed by Lexiom or counterpart player: ◯
- Player approved, awaiting counterpart approval: ring with check (◯ with ✓ rendered inside the ring)

---

## 8. Temporal Model — Turn-Based

White A → Black → White B → Black

No simultaneous interaction.

---

## 9. Round Structure

### Round 1 — Stabilization

Goal:

- emotional de-escalation
- overlapping interests
- first jointly tolerable semantic substrate

### Round 2 and beyond — Resolution / Convergence

Goal:

- work through open gaps
- evaluate options
- converge on negotiable formulations and possible agreements

Accord may involve more than one round.

---

## 10. Recalculated Shared Case Seed

At the beginning of each new round, Lexiom recalculates the operative **shared case seed**.

### Source for recalculation

The recalculated shared case seed must be inferred from:

- the current contents of the **shared documents** section only

### Excluded from recalculation

The recalculated shared case seed must not be inferred from:

- staged-only artifacts
- unapproved counterpart drafts
- private notes or private home-run materials not yet promoted to shared

### Purpose

This ensures that each round begins from:

- mutually accepted semantic ground
- the latest stable overlap
- a current map of what is jointly carried forward

This creates a **forward-only shared reality spine** across rounds.

---

## 11. Round 1 Flow — L2-Aligned Semantic Progression

Round 1 follows a structured sequence aligned with Lexiom’s L2 semantic topics.

Player B completes an initial private home run before shared synthesis intensifies.

Throughout all steps:

- Lexiom performs continuous listening and reflection
  - via chat responses
  - via analysis of user edits on draft proposals
- the system responds with adaptive reframing
- goal: the user feels
  → “Lexiom truly understands my point of view”

### Step 1 — Identification of Disputed Items

Output:
→ **Approved Dispute Set (L2a)**

### Step 2 — Personal Goals Definition

Output:
→ **Approved Goals (L2b)**

### Step 3 — Personal Strategy Definition

Output:
→ **Approved Strategy (L2c)**

### Step 4 — Identification of Undisputed Items

Output:
→ **Approved Undisputed Anchors (L2d)**

---

## 12. Player B Home Run Completion

Once all four steps are approved, Player B completes a private semantic home run.

Lexiom then proposes an action and generates its bounded artifact.

That bounded artifact:

- is approved by Player B
- is placed in the **staged** section of the right panel
- awaits Player A approval before promotion into **shared**

---

## 13. Staged Artifact Generation

Based on Player B’s approved private inputs and the current shared seed, GT3 synthesizes a new artifact:

→ **Interest-Focused Shared Narrative**

Characteristics:

- derived from Player B’s perspective
- constrained by current shared ground
- structured around interests and overlaps
- expressed in third-perspective language

---

## 14. Transition to Shared Evaluation

The synthesized artifact, once approved by Player B, is presented to Player A in Player A’s **staged** section.

Player A may:

- review
- approve
- decline approval and provide structured feedback

Player A may not directly edit this artifact.

---

## 15. Gate Condition for Shared Promotion

A staged artifact becomes a **shared artifact** only if the counterpart approves it.

If the counterpart does not approve it and continues the turn without approval, Lexiom Accord enforces a **mediated convergence protocol**.

---

## 16. Mediated Convergence Protocol

### Rule 1 — No Direct Counterpart Editing

Counterparts may not directly rewrite each other’s counterpart-facing staged shared drafts.

Counterparts may only:

- approve
- submit structured feedback

### Rule 2 — Structured Feedback Instead of Redrafting

Feedback must be constrained to analyzable semantic categories such as:

- what feels accurate
- what feels inaccurate
- what feels missing
- what already feels acceptable

### Rule 3 — Black-Only Re-Synthesis

After receiving relevant feedback and approved materials, Lexiom Accord infers GT3 to produce the next version of the staged artifact.

### Rule 4 — Preserve Accepted Fragments

Any fragment already accepted by both parties must persist unless both explicitly reopen it.

### Rule 5 — Common-Ground Fallback

If convergence is weak, Lexiom reduces the draft to:

- overlapping interests
- mutually tolerable formulations
- open questions still requiring clarification

### Rule 6 — Escalation Dampening

Expected synthesis behavior should:

- lower accusatory specificity
- increase neutral abstraction
- shift from positions toward interests
- avoid amplifying emotionally charged wording

---

## 17. Semantic Outcome of Each Round

Each round should produce:

- an updated shared-document set
- a recalculated shared case seed for the next round
- a clearer distinction between:
  - accepted overlap
  - unresolved gaps
  - next focus topics

Round 1 especially aims to end with:

- a jointly intelligible summary of overlapping interests
- a map of remaining gaps
- a stable starting point for Round 2

---

## 18. Design Essence

Accord proceeds through:

> mutually approved semantic anchors,
> staged convergence over unresolved drafts,
> and round-by-round recalculation of the shared case seed from the shared document set.
