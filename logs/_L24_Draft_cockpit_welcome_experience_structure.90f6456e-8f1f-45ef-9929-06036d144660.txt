Center playfield — showcase one selected OSN section at a time as a Lexiom draft-first card. This card may display Seed, Thematic Lenses, Output Spec, Success Evidences, or a generated Build card for the selected OSN, serving as the sole space for review, editing, and decisive interaction.

Top L2 panel — highlight the selected OSN's thematic lenses as active chips. Clicking a lens selects it, prompting a GT3-backed reframe of the current center section through that lens. For child OSNs like UX.osn and CodeShape.osn, the GT3 request includes ancestor context, referencing each ancestor's seed and full output_spec, starting with the direct parent, ProductWelcome.osn. The result appears as an unapproved draft in the center playfield, with approval remaining a distinct action. While a lens is active, the focus row displays the lens name alongside the current section. Build sections disable lens interaction, maintaining clarity.

Right panel — dedicated to demoable evidence artifacts linked to the selected OSN's success evidences. In the absence of real artifacts, a placeholder state is shown rather than the full evidence-definition text, inviting future contributions.

Bottom ribbon — serves as a visible placeholder region; specific semantic actions will be defined in later design iterations, allowing for organic growth.

Selection behavior — selecting an OSN or one of its section/build children updates the center playfield and L2 focus. Clicking the OSN row toggles the visibility of its section list. The selected OSN's title, owner, discipline, and current section remain visible, ensuring users are always aware of their context.

Functional minimum:
- Display all five cockpit regions upon initial load.
- Allow users to select each of the four OSNs from the left graph.
- Begin with all OSN section lists collapsed, fostering exploration.
- Enable selection of section children for the highlighted OSN.
- Permit clicking an L2 thematic lens to receive a GT3-backed draft reframe in the center card.
- Allow users to invoke build from a per-OSN glyph, revealing a Build child.
- Present the bottom ribbon as an empty or lightly labeled placeholder.
